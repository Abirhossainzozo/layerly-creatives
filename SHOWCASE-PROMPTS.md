# Showcase prompts

Ten showcase creatives for the Layerly Creatives page and for testing the skill. Each one has two
parts:

1. **Image prompt** for ChatGPT (or any image model). Generates the visual only. No text, no logos,
   fictional people, blue palette.
2. **Build brief** for the skill. Takes the generated image, adds every word as an editable text
   layer, delivers the layered PSD.

Palette: primary blue `#1F5EFF`, deep navy `#0B1B3A`, ice `#EAF0FF`, accent yellow `#FFD23F`
(CTAs only), white text. All brands below are fictional.

How to run one: generate the image, upload it to a chat that has the skill, paste the build brief,
ask for the preview first, then the PSD.

---

## 01 Weekly deals flyer (Nila Mart)

**Image prompt (4:5):**
Fresh produce photography for a grocery flyer: green and yellow plantains, red onions in a net bag,
a rice sack, canned beans, on a rustic wooden table with soft blue morning light and blue cloth,

"WEEKEND SUPER SAVER", strip "Validity: Fri to Sun". 5x2 product grid using the isolated product
images, titles and prices: Green plantain 3 for $2 · Yellow plantain $1 · Red onion 5kg $6.99 ·
Basmati rice 5kg $8.50 · Kidney beans 1kg $3.49 · Snack mix 2 for $5 · Frozen paratha 2 for $14 ·
Assorted snacks 2 for $6 · Crispy bhujia $5.99 · Whole fish $22/kg. Footer: phone +1 234 567 890,
open 10am to midnight, 12 Market Street. Preview, QA, then PSD.

### Notes for testers

- Every prompt says "no text": image models misspell. All words are added by the skill as editable
  type layers, which is the point of the product.
- People are fictional. Never use a real person's likeness in showcase material.
- If a generated image has a hard rectangle edge against the design, add a bottom `fade` mask.
- Run the QA line and the audit sheet before calling a showcase done.
